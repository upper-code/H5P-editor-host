H5P Hub client and dependency notices
====================================

Locally restored notices (2026-10-30). The shipped h5p-hub-client.js matches
h5p/h5p-editor-php-library commit d1956245cd85c529b23202e38fe48535c1011ba2.
Its SHA-256 is 7b73f5dfc93fdd341c0b48f1a8c069f2412bb4105d63891c746ddc1217aea848.

The dependency license texts below come from the published npm packages in
the upstream Hub client's dependency snapshot at commit
5330aad07d298a0bd3f1ab6bcdfb84f57c549757. Build helpers and dependencies may
be inlined or removed by the bundler; their notices are retained here too.
This is a restoration of the notice set, not a claim of a verified rebuild.
